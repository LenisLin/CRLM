### **Multi-Modal Analysis Schedule: Integrating ST and IMC for Vascular Niche Analysis**

**Project Goal:** To test the hypothesis that specific malignant cell subtypes ("seeds") co-localize with vascular niches, and that this interaction, combined with a "permissive" peritumor microenvironment ("soil"), drives recurrence.

---

### **Phase 1: Vascular Landscape Discovery and Proxy Validation (Weeks 1-2)**
*Focus: Leverage the unbiased ST data to define vascular patterns and validate a proxy marker for IMC.*

**Week 1: Task 1 - Identify Vascular Hotspots in Spatial Transcriptomics**
* **Objective**: To map the vascular architecture in the ST samples.
* **Primary Dataset**: 9 spatial transcriptomics (ST) samples.
* **Key Analytical Methods**:
    1.  Create an "Endothelial Cell" gene signature score using canonical markers (e.g., `PECAM1`, `VWF`, `CDH5`, `ENG`).
    2.  Apply this signature score to each spot in the ST data to quantify vascular density.
    3.  Visualize the scores as a "vascular heatmap" on top of the H&E images for each sample.
* **Expected Output/Target**: For each of the 9 ST samples, a spatial map identifying "vascular hotspots" (spots with high endothelial signature scores).

**Week 2: Task 2 - Validate `VEGF` as a Vascular Proxy and Link Vessels to Tumor Phenotypes**
* **Objective**: (A) To confirm that `VEGF` expression can be used as a reliable proxy for vascular proximity in the IMC data. (B) To identify which tumor cell phenotypes are spatially associated with these vessels.
* **Primary Dataset**: 9 ST samples and your scRNA-seq reference atlas.
* **Key Analytical Methods**:
    1.  **Proxy Validation**: In the ST data, calculate the spatial correlation (e.g., using Moran's I or Pearson correlation of co-located spots) between the "Endothelial Cell" signature score (from Task 1) and the normalized expression of the `VEGF` gene.
    2.  **Tumor Phenotype Deconvolution**: Use your scRNA-seq reference to deconvolve the ST spots and estimate the fractional abundance of your tumor subtypes (e.g., "$GLUT1$-like", "$FASN$-like", "$EpCAM$-quiescent").
    3.  **Vessel-Tumor Correlation**: Calculate the spatial correlation between the "Endothelial Cell" signature and the abundance of each deconvolved tumor subtype.
* **Expected Output/Target**:
    * A strong, statistically significant positive correlation between the endothelial signature and `VEGF` gene expression, validating `VEGF` as a proxy.
    * A report stating which tumor cell subtype(s) are significantly co-located with vascular hotspots (e.g., "The $GLUT1$-like malignant cell program shows a significant spatial association with vascular hotspots in the ST data").

### **Phase 2: "Seed" Analysis in the Full IMC Cohort (Weeks 3-4)**
*Focus: Apply the validated proxy to the larger, clinically-annotated IMC cohort to analyze the "seed's" metastatic potential.*

**Week 3: Task 3 - Characterize the "Vaso-Invasive Seed" using the `VEGF` Proxy**
* **Objective**: To identify and phenotype tumor cells residing in pro-angiogenic niches in the full IMC cohort.
* **Primary Dataset**: 35-patient IMC cohort (focus on IM region).
* **Key Analytical Methods**:
    1.  Define **"VEGF-Hotspots"** in the IMC data (e.g., regions with `VEGF` protein expression above the 75th percentile).
    2.  Define tumor cells within a specific radius (e.g., 20µm) of these hotspots as the **"Vaso-Invasive Seed"**.
    3.  Compare the full protein expression profile of these "Vaso-Invasive" tumor cells to other tumor cells. Quantify enrichment of markers like `GLUT1`, `Ki67`, and `Vimentin`.
* **Expected Output/Target**: A statistical summary showing that tumor cells near `VEGF`-hotspots are phenotypically distinct and enriched for aggressive markers. An RFS survival analysis comparing patients with a high vs. low percentage of "Vaso-Invasive Seeds."

**Week 4: Task 4 - Quantify the Peritumor "Soil" Composition**
* **Objective**: To characterize the immune and stromal context of the peritumor (PT) region for each patient.
* **Primary Dataset**: 35-patient IMC cohort (focus on PT region).
* **Key Analytical Methods**:
    1.  UnBaised analysis the difference between R and NR -> highlight the ECs
    2.  explore the co-localization of EC with Stromal and Immune cell
    3.  construct and explore the microenvrionment of bile duct cells
    4.  construct the biomarker 
    5.  clinical transition
* **Expected Output/Target**: For each of the 35 patients, a set of "Soil" metrics (e.g., Treg/CD8 ratio, immune exclusion distance, CAF density). A survival analysis showing the prognostic value of these soil metrics alone.

### **Phase 3: Integrated Seed-and-Soil Modeling (Weeks 5-6)**
*Focus: Synthesize all findings into a final, integrated model that predicts patient outcomes.*

**Week 5-6: Task 5 - Build and Test the Integrated "Seed-Soil" Interaction Model**
* **Objective**: To test the central hypothesis that recurrence is driven by the interaction between a high-potential "seed" and a "permissive soil."
* **Primary Dataset**: All data from the 35-patient IMC cohort.
* **Key Analytical Methods**:
    1.  **Patient Stratification**: Create a "High-Risk Seed" group (e.g., patients with a high percentage of "Vaso-Invasive Seeds" from Task 3) and a "Low-Risk Seed" group.
    2.  **Interaction Analysis**: Within the "High-Risk Seed" group, further stratify patients by their "Soil" metrics (e.g., "Permissive Soil" [High Treg/CD8] vs. "Hostile Soil" [Low Treg/CD8]).
    3.  **Survival Modeling**: Generate Kaplan-Meier curves for these four groups (High-Risk Seed/Permissive Soil, High-Risk Seed/Hostile Soil, etc.). Use a Cox Proportional Hazards model with an interaction term (`RFS ~ Seed_Risk * Soil_Risk`) to formally test the synergy.
* **Expected Output/Target**: A final set of survival plots and statistical results demonstrating that patients with both a "High-Risk Seed" and a "Permissive Soil" have a significantly worse prognosis than all other groups, providing strong evidence for your hypothesis.
